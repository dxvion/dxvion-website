PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Dxvion Lifesciences Private Limited respects the privacy of people who contact us through this website. This policy explains, in general terms, how information submitted through dxvion.com may be handled.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry or contact us by email, we may receive information such as your name, email address, company, country, enquiry type and any details you include in your message.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to business requests, discuss products or partnerships, provide relevant commercial information, coordinate follow-up and maintain appropriate business records.

Website form processing

Website form submissions are processed through a third-party form service so that your enquiry can be delivered to Dxvion. Service providers may process information on our behalf only as needed to provide their service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with appropriate service providers, manufacturers, business partners or professional advisers where reasonably necessary to respond to an enquiry, progress a legitimate business request or comply with applicable law.

Retention and security

We keep business enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, for follow-up, record-keeping or legal requirements. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your questions

If you have a privacy question or want us to review information you previously submitted, contact info@dxvion.com.
